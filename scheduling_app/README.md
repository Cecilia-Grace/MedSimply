# scheduling_app

The **scheduling_app** manages medication schedules and medication administration within the MedSimply system. It ensures that medications are given on time, to the correct patient, and only by the assigned health worker.

Purpose
* Enforce safe and timely medication administration
* Ensure staff accountability
* Maintain accurate medication records

Resposibilities
* Create and manage medication schedules
* Assign schedules to health workers
* Track scheduled vs given medication
* Record who administered medication and when

Models
* **Schedule** – medication, patient, assigned health worker, scheduled time, given status
* **NotificationLog** – records reminders and alerts sent
